# Hook examples

Short example lines from the sources, with why they work or fail. Keys are in the Sources table of
`SKILL.md`. View counts are what the creator reported.

## Weak, then fixed

| Weak | Fixed | Fix | Key |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Guys, this is one of the craziest things I've ever seen..." | "Here are three simple ways to improve your gut health" | context in second 1 | K |
| "These guys built a $30 million empire and the online money they made is most difficult to earn if you don't develop a journaling practice" | "These guys built a $30 million empire. Their secret for earning money online was their insane journaling practice." | same idea, clear order | K |
| "I've struggled with skin problems my whole life" | "If you've struggled with skin problems your whole life..." | "you", not "I" | K |
| "These are three common trends in skincare" | "If you struggle with acne, try these three things" | need-to-have pain | K |
| "Magnesium is one of 21 core minerals" | "If you want better sleep, you need to be taking magnesium" | benefit before subject | K |
| "I definitely don't cut out carbs" | "Lean at 45 and I eat carbs every day" | context + belief challenge | HD |
| "Who can relate?" | (name the need and a question) | no need, no question | HD |
| "[Unknown name] made a nearly unplayable game" | "This guy created the worst game ever" | no unknown name, no hedge | IG |
| a flat record as headline | "This is the unluckiest 18-year-old in American history" | character first, record later | IG |
| "I tried Filipino food" | "I flew 7,292 mi to eat here" (title) | specific, raises questions | AA |
| "Develop better habits" | "Here's how to break your shitty habits" | how people actually talk | ML |
| "This is how you get people addicted to your content. It's called dopamine ladders..." (graded B) | "Most creators trigger one type of dopamine in their videos. The addictive ones use a six-level ladder." | no soft opener, contrast, specific | K |

## By archetype (K)

- Fortune teller: "That new microwave is going to change cooking forever."
- Experimenter: "Does anything look weird about this shot to you?" (smart-glasses demo, ~50M)
- Teacher: "Three things you can learn from Aritzia."
- Magician: "Check this out." (signature opener used for ~30 videos)
- Investigator: "This is a secret Japanese city built at the base of Mount Fuji."
- Contrarian: "You have no creative ideas because your space sucks to live in."
- Same topic, two archetypes: "This backpack is going to completely change the way millennials
  travel" / "Everybody loves this bag, but if you use it every day, it is so overhyped."

## Full hooks that worked

- "The tech in the Vegas Sphere is insane, 20 times bigger than an IMAX. But get this: the screen
  is the least impressive part..." (then: the audio) — context lean, interjection, snapback. 8M. K
- "These are called life-size floor plans. You can literally walk through your exact home design
  before you build it." — visual carries the contrast; text + arrow on screen. 15M. K
- "If we were smarter, we would listen to 13-year-olds more." — contrast from an unexpected
  object. 2.9M. K
- "Do you know why Mr. Beast turned down $1 billion?" — famous name + huge number + why. 12M. DB
- "This coffee should not taste good..." with the key ingredient blurred — loop kept open. HD
- "A pilot had a seizure mid-flight and the plane started going down." — built-in stakes. IG
- "Despite this high school senior being diagnosed with leukemia..." — turn word forces a "then
  what". IG
- "My grandma thinks Christmas is expensive, so I'm going to prove her wrong by giving her the best
  Christmas present with $5." — idea + built-in payoff. LS
- "Here's how to stop waking up hating what you see" — one structure reused to grow an account
  0 to 100K in a month. ML
- "If you've been lying in bed scrolling for the past hour..." — names the viewer's exact
  situation. ML

## Text hooks (on-screen, K)

- "Make your hooks unskippable" — dream outcome.
- "Claude just changed social media forever" — subject + something just changed.
- "I made $97,000 in 13 days on Instagram" — specific beats "a few thousand in a few weeks".
- Line break keeps phrases whole: "ChatGPT just pioneered / the future of work".

## Flops the creators admitted

- "This is the future of storytelling. It's called a generative world model." — abstract, jargon,
  nothing on screen to match; under 100K. K
- "Have you ever experienced a weird feeling that something has already happened before..." —
  abstract concept, weak visuals; 30K. DB
