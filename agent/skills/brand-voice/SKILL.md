# Brand voice: call to action and address

The channel is **macroBody** (`@macrobody.fr`), the French nutrition and macro-tracking
app. These rules come from the owner (brand kit `brand/brand.json`, blocks `cta` and
`address`). An owner learning that says otherwise wins.

## Call to action

- The offer is the free trial: **"7 jours gratuits"** (or "7 jours gratuits, sans
  engagement"), then the site **"macrobody.fr"**. When there is room, add
  **"Dispo sur App Store & Google Play"**.
- **Never show or say a price**, a discount or a promo code: not on screen, not in the
  voice, not in the caption.
- **Never write "lien en bio"** (or "link in bio") unless an owner learning asks for it.
  Point to macrobody.fr instead.
- One CTA per video, at the end (end card or last line), never in the hook. Every video
  ends on its style's animated end card, which draws the CTA itself (the mB tile, the
  wordmark, the trial pill, the site): you only give its **tagline** (one short line tied
  to the payoff, never the CTA again), its **accent** (the topic's macro colour) and, where
  the template has one, its **cameo** (a character or food from the story). The
  `end_card` recipe op takes the same `text` (tagline), `style` (`live` over the clip's
  last frame, `clean` on the light surface) and `accent`.

## Tu or vous

The address depends on the template family:

- **vous** (the default): informative and brand videos (facts, myths, quiz, stories that
  teach) and every format not listed below.
- **tu**: humour (POV, sketches such as PixelSkit, any idea in a humour pillar) and
  live-action videos built on real footage (remixes, reactions).

Keep one address for the whole video: hook, voice, captions, end card and caption text.
